We moved in March. I keep meaning to write about it and then not doing it, so here, badly, is the thing.

The flat is smaller. Objectively worse on paper — less light, a kitchen you have to turn sideways in, and the boiler makes a noise at 6am that I have decided to interpret as character. But it's four minutes from the park and I'm outside more than I have been in years, so.

Packing was awful. I don't mean "stressful" in the way people say it, I mean I sat on the floor of the old place at 1am surrounded by boxes of stuff I hadn't touched since the last move and genuinely could not work out why I owned three woks. Three. I don't cook.

The movers were great. The van was not. It broke down on the North Circular with everything I own inside it and we sat on the verge for two and a half hours eating service station sandwiches while a man called Dave explained the fuel system to me in detail I did not ask for and could not follow. Dave was lovely. Dave's van was a disgrace.

What I didn't expect: how long it takes to stop reaching for things in the wrong place. Months. I still turn the wrong way out of the bedroom at night. My hands haven't caught up with the floorplan.

What I did expect but underestimated: the admin. Council tax, the energy switch, the post redirect that worked for about nine days then silently stopped. Someone somewhere has my bank statements.

Would I do it again? Not for a while. Ask me in five years when I've forgotten the woks.
